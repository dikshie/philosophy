# Philosophy Study Workspace

A structured 1-Year Self-Study program in fundamental Philosophy tailored for backgrounds in **Physics** and **Computer Science**, using exclusively the peer-reviewed [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy (SEP)](https://plato.stanford.edu/).

## Structure & Schedule

- **Time Commitment**: 2 hours/day, 5 days/week (Monday–Friday).
- **Duration**: 52 Weeks (~48 active weeks, 4 holiday/consolidation buffer weeks).
- **Core Plan**: See [`STUDY_PLAN.md`](STUDY_PLAN.md) for the complete weekly and daily curriculum.

### The Four Quarters

1. **Quarter 1: Logic, Computability, and Epistemology (Weeks 1–12)**
   - Classical & Modal Logic, Computability, Incompleteness, Information Theory, Justification, Bayesian Epistemology, Scientific Method & Realism.
2. **Quarter 2: Metaphysics, Time, and Philosophy of Physics (Weeks 13–24)**
   - Ontology, Identity, Mereology, Causality, Determinism, Space-Time Relationism, Statistical Mechanics, Quantum Measurement Interpretations, Structural Realism.
3. **Quarter 3: Philosophy of Mind, Language, and AI (Weeks 25–36)**
   - Dualism vs. Physicalism, Functionalism, Consciousness & Qualia, Intentionality, Chinese Room, Frege/Russell/Wittgenstein, Logical Empiricism, AI Ethics.
4. **Quarter 4: Ethics, Political Philosophy, and the Western Canon (Weeks 37–48)**
   - Metaethics, Utilitarianism, Kantian Deontology, Virtue Ethics, Social Contract, Philosophy of Technology, Presocratics, Plato, Aristotle, Early Modern Rationalists & Empiricists, Kant.
5. **Weeks 49–52: Integrated Capstone & Year-End Synthesis**
   - Synthesis essays integrating physical foundations, computational limits, and philosophical argumentation.
